Case Study: Brand Identity

DELANEEN SYSTEM
OBJECTIVE

Building recognition baseline

Big brands require extreme consistency. The baseline objective for Delaneen was to develop an interlocking visual corporate guidelines model representing professional excellence across both physical stationery cards and high-performance browser interfaces.

DESIGN SYSTEM

Aesthetics & Typographic Logic

We structured a robust design system baseline based on standard geometric rules. Color swatches utilize warm corporate sand-beige contrasted with electric neon highlights, creating a sense of brutalist technology and high-end creative direction.

  • Pantone Matching System
  • Scalable Vector Geometry
  • Letterhead & Collateral
  • Full PDF Brand Guidelines
OUTCOME

Enterprise Brand Readiness

The brand architecture successfully established a memorable visual footprint. All materials scale seamlessly from A4 letterhead forms down to 16px desktop tab icons, ensuring complete brand readiness for massive commercial markets.

Case Study: Logo Design

HIGH VOLTAGE LOGOMARK
OBJECTIVE

Extreme visual recall at any scale

The brief was simple: craft a brand mark that stops scrolling, looks incredible at 16px favicon size, and maintains its presence at billboard scale. Every curve and counter-space was intentionally calculated.

APPROACH

Geometric precision & typographic harmony

Starting from a geometric grid system, I explored over 30 sketch directions before narrowing to three strong candidates. The final mark blends angular energy with refined letterform proportions, tested across dark/light backgrounds and multiple size contexts.

  • 30+ initial sketch explorations
  • Grid-based geometric construction
  • Multi-context size testing (16px → billboard)
  • Dark/light background validation
OUTCOME

A mark built for versatility

The final logo system scales flawlessly from 16px tab icons to large-format prints. Delivered as a complete vector suite (AI, SVG, EPS, PNG) with monochrome and color variants ready for immediate deployment across digital and physical media.

Case Study: Event Flyer

ELECTRIC NEON BASH
OBJECTIVE

Driving virality & conversions

The goal was to design a flyer so visually electric that people would share it without being asked. Every element — from the neon glow palette to the typography weight — was tuned to maximize engagement and drive ticket bookings.

APPROACH

Neon-brutalist visual strategy

I combined electric neon color treatments with bold, retro-brutalist typography to create instant visual tension. The layout uses aggressive spatial hierarchy to guide the eye from headline to date to call-to-action in under 2 seconds of viewing time.

  • Electric neon color grading
  • Retro-brutalist typography pairing
  • 2-second scan-path optimization
  • Social-native format sizing
OUTCOME

Viral organic sharing

The flyer was optimized for Instagram Stories, WhatsApp forwards, and print handouts. Its striking neon palette generated significant organic reach, with the design being re-shared across multiple social platforms without any paid promotion.

Case Study: Cyberpunk Poster

CYBERPUNK TYPOGRAPHY COMP
OBJECTIVE

Museum-quality visual layout

APPROACH

OUTCOME

Gallery-grade visual impact

Case Study: Social Campaign

SOCIAL MEDIA CAMPAIGN SET
OBJECTIVE

Consistent brand aesthetics

Brands lose trust when their social presence looks fragmented. This project required building a cohesive visual system that maintains identity coherence across Instagram, Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and WhatsApp Status — without looking repetitive.

APPROACH

Modular template system

I designed a modular grid system where every post, story, and banner shares the same foundational layout DNA but allows for variation in content and imagery. Color tokens, typography scales, and spacing rules were locked to the brand guide.

  • Modular grid-based template system
  • Platform-specific sizing (IG, X, LinkedIn)
  • Brand token enforcement (colors, type, spacing)
  • Reusable Figma component library
OUTCOME

Scalable engagement framework

The final deliverable was a complete social media kit with editable templates, a brand style guide for social, and ready-to-post assets. The modular system allows the client to produce new on-brand content independently, reducing ongoing design costs.
