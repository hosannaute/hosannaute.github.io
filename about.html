HOSANNA UTE
DESIGNER & CURIOUS BUILDER

I Design. I Build.
I Keep Learning.

Lagos, Nigeria
I’m Hosanna. I like strong typography, a good visual idea, and the moment something I’ve made starts working. Sometimes that’s a brand identity. Sometimes it’s a website or a small game.

I study Computer Engineering at the University of Ilorin, and I create under the name Delaneen Design. Graphic and web design are where I feel most at home. Code gives me another way to follow an idea and see where it goes.

WHO I AM

A designer who gets curious

I started learning JavaScript on my phone. I like figuring things out with what I have, trying an idea, getting stuck, and working out the next step. Making something gives me a reason to keep learning.

Games are part of that. I enjoy making a set of rules turn into something you can play, then adjusting the details until it feels right. Design and code meet quite naturally there.

CAPABILITIES

What I Bring

I work on graphic design, visual identities, web layouts, and interfaces. I use HTML, CSS, and JavaScript for the web, and I have Python and Pandas knowledge. I’m learning Node.js and Express as I build my backend foundation.

Graphic Design Web Design HTML5 & CSS3 JavaScript Figma UI/UX Python & Pandas Node.js & Express (learning)
BACKGROUND

More than one way to make things

I’m drawn to the bold type and raw edges of Neo Brutalism, but my creativity moves around. I play piano, knit, enjoy fashion, and write. In secondary school, I wrote a 90-page novella. Making things has been a constant; the medium keeps changing.

EXPERIENCE & EDUCATION

What I’m building towards

My Computer Engineering studies sit alongside the things I learn for myself. An IT placement at Coca-Cola Nigeria in Lagos has also been part of that experience. I’m interested in how the pieces of a system work together, beyond what appears on a screen.

Backend and cloud engineering are the direction I want to grow in. Right now, that means getting comfortable with Node.js, Express, and the basics of requests, responses, and APIs. I want the understanding to come from things I can build and explain.
